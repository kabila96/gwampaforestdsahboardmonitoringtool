THE STORY BEHIND THE MAPS

🏚️

When people invade forests...

Paths become fields, temporary shelters become permanent pressure, and tree cover is cleared for farming, firewood and charcoal.

🌡️

Degradation becomes heat

Reduced canopy weakens cooling. Bare soil absorbs more heat, raising LST and increasing fire vulnerability.

🎯

Evidence becomes action

Priority zones are where forest loss, high LST and weak burn recovery overlap.
