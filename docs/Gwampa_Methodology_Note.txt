Gwampa Forest Monitoring Dashboard - Methodology Note

Created by Powell Ndlovu, Zimbabwe.

Workflow summary:
1. Gwampa Forest boundary was used to define the area of interest.
2. Google Earth Engine was used to process annual remote sensing indicators.
3. Esri Living Atlas land cover layers were included for 2018-2025 land cover evidence.
4. Annual statistics include NDVI, LST, NBR / burn ratio, annual forest loss, and cumulative forest loss.
5. Dashboard charts and map panels communicate degradation, surface heat stress, burn recovery and restoration priorities.

Competition evidence:
- Stipulated datasets represented: Esri Living Atlas and RCOE / RCMRD Geoportal sources.
- Processing platform: Google Earth Engine.
